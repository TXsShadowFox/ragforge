NORTHFIELD COLLEGE IT SERVICES - FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

How do I connect to the campus Wi-Fi?
Connect to the network called NFC-Secure and log in with your college email address and
password. The guest network NFC-Guest is for visitors only: it is slower and disconnects every
4 hours. Each student can connect up to 3 devices at the same time.

How do I reset my password?
Go to password.northfield.edu and follow the steps. You will receive a code on the mobile
number in your student record. Passwords must have at least 12 characters and must be changed
every 180 days. After 5 wrong login attempts, your account is locked for 30 minutes.

Where is the IT Help Desk?
The IT Help Desk is in the Library Building, ground floor, room G-14. It is open from 8 am to
8 pm on weekdays and from 10 am to 2 pm on Saturdays. You can also call extension 4357 or
write to helpdesk@northfield.edu. Most requests are answered within one working day.

Which software can I get for free?
All students get Microsoft 365 with 1 TB of OneDrive storage while they are enrolled. MATLAB
and SPSS are available on the computers in the labs and through the virtual desktop service.
Students in the engineering programs can request a free AutoCAD student license from the IT
Help Desk.

How much email storage do I have?
Student mailboxes have 50 GB of storage. Your college email account stays active for 6 months
after you graduate; after that, it is deleted. Forward important messages to a personal
address before then.

Can I use the computer labs?
The computer labs in the Engineering Block are open from 8 am to 9 pm on weekdays. Lab 2 is
reserved for classes until 1 pm. Printing in the labs uses the same account as the library
printers.

What should I do about a phishing email?
Do not click any link in it. Forward it to security@northfield.edu and delete it. IT Services
will never ask for your password by email.

How do I get help with my laptop?
The IT Help Desk checks student laptops for free, including virus removal and operating
system installation. Hardware repairs are not done on campus; the Help Desk can give you a
list of authorized repair shops.

How do I use the virtual desktop?
Go to vdesk.northfield.edu and log in with your college account. The virtual desktop gives
access to the lab software from your own computer, from 6 am to midnight. Each session ends
after 3 hours of no activity.
