Residence Hall Rules

These rules apply to all students living in the four Northfield College residence halls: Cedar Hall, Maple Hall, Oak Hall and Pine Hall.

Gates and visitors

The main gates of the residence halls close at 10:30 pm on weekdays and at 11:30 pm on Fridays and Saturdays. Students who come back after the gates close must sign the late-entry register at the security desk. Visitors may stay in the common rooms between 9 am and 8 pm. Visitors are not allowed in the bedrooms at any time. Every visitor must show a photo ID at the security desk and leave before 8 pm.

Rooms

Rooms are shared by two students, except in Pine Hall, where postgraduate students have single rooms. Each room has two beds, two desks, a wardrobe and a ceiling fan. Students may not move the furniture out of the room. Cooking appliances such as rice cookers and electric stoves are not allowed in the rooms because of the fire risk; a shared kitchen is on every floor. Room inspections take place once a month, with 24 hours of notice.

Keys and access cards

Each student gets one room key and one access card. A lost room key costs 400 rupees to replace, and a lost access card costs 250 rupees. If you are locked out after 10:30 pm, the security desk can open your room once per semester for free; after that, each opening costs 100 rupees.

Laundry

Washing machines are in the basement of each hall. A wash costs 30 rupees and a dryer cycle costs 20 rupees, paid with the access card. The laundry rooms are open from 6 am to 11 pm.

Quiet hours

Quiet hours are from 10 pm to 7 am every day. During the final exam weeks, quiet hours last all day. Music must not be heard outside the room at any time.

Moving in and out

Move-in day for the autumn semester is 30 August. Students must leave their rooms within 3 days after their last exam of the spring semester. A refundable deposit of 5,000 rupees is paid when moving in; it is returned within 30 days after moving out, minus the cost of any damage.

Hostel fees

The housing fee is 36,000 rupees per year for a shared room and 48,000 rupees for a single room. The mess (dining) plan costs an extra 30,000 rupees per year and is required for all residents of Cedar Hall and Maple Hall.

Complaints and repairs

Report repairs through the housing portal. Urgent problems, such as a water leak or a power cut, can be reported at the security desk at any time. The Housing Office is in Oak Hall, ground floor, and is open from 10 am to 5 pm on weekdays.
