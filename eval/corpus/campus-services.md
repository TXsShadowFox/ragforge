# Campus Services at Northfield College

## Dining

The main cafeteria in the Student Centre serves breakfast from 7:30 am to 9:30 am, lunch from
12 pm to 2:30 pm, and dinner from 7 pm to 9:30 pm. A vegetarian thali costs 90 rupees. The
Coffee Corner next to the Central Library is open from 8 am to 8 pm and sells snacks, tea and
coffee. Students on the mess plan eat in the Cedar Hall dining room.

## Health Centre

The Health Centre is behind the Sports Complex. A doctor is available from 9 am to 5 pm on
weekdays, and a nurse is on duty 24 hours a day. Consultations are free for students;
medicines from the campus pharmacy are sold at a 20 percent discount. For emergencies at
night, call the campus ambulance at extension 2222. Every student must have health
insurance; the college plan costs 2,500 rupees per year and covers hospital stays up to 2
lakh rupees.

## Counselling

The Counselling Centre offers free and confidential sessions with trained counsellors. Book
a session by email at counselling@northfield.edu or walk in between 2 pm and 5 pm on
weekdays. Students can have up to 8 sessions per semester.

## Sports Complex

The Sports Complex has a swimming pool, a gym, two badminton courts and a cricket ground.
The gym is open from 6 am to 10 pm and the pool from 7 am to 7 pm. A sports membership costs
1,200 rupees per semester; swimming lessons cost an extra 800 rupees. The cricket ground can
be booked by student clubs through the Sports Office.

## Transport

Free college buses run between the campus and the city railway station every 30 minutes
from 7 am to 9 pm on weekdays. On weekends, the buses run every hour. Students can rent
bicycles from the Student Centre for 20 rupees per day. Parking a car on campus requires a
parking permit, which costs 3,000 rupees per year.

## Lost and found

Items found on campus are kept at the security office in the Main Building for 60 days.
After that, unclaimed items are given to charity. To claim an item, bring your student ID
card.

## Student clubs

There are more than 40 student clubs, from photography to robotics. Clubs present
themselves at the Club Fair in the second week of September. To start a new club, you need
at least 15 members and a faculty adviser.
