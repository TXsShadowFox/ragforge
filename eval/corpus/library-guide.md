# Northfield College Library Guide

The Northfield College Library has two buildings: the Central Library next to the Main
Building, and the Science Library in the Science Block. This guide explains how to borrow
books, use the study rooms, and get help with research.

## Opening hours

The Central Library is open from 8 am to 10 pm from Monday to Saturday, and from 10 am to
6 pm on Sunday. During the final exam weeks, it stays open until midnight. The Science
Library is open from 9 am to 7 pm on weekdays and is closed on weekends. Both libraries
close on national holidays.

## Library card

Your student ID card is also your library card. Activate it at the Central Library front
desk in your first week. If you lose your card, report it at once: you are responsible for
any items borrowed with it until you report the loss. A replacement card costs 200 rupees.

## Borrowing

Undergraduate students can borrow up to 6 books at a time. Postgraduate students can borrow
up to 10 books. The loan period is 14 days for most books. You can renew a book twice online
through the library portal, as long as no other student has reserved it. Reference books,
newspapers and theses cannot be borrowed; they must be used inside the library.

Books from the Short Loan Collection can be borrowed for 24 hours only. These are textbooks
that many students need at the same time, and they are kept behind the front desk.

## Late returns and fines

A book returned after its due date costs 5 rupees per day. For Short Loan books, the fine is
20 rupees per hour. Fines are added to your library account, and you cannot borrow new books
while you owe more than 100 rupees. A lost book must be paid for at its current price plus a
processing fee of 150 rupees.

## Laptops and equipment

The Central Library lends laptops for use inside the building. A laptop can be borrowed for
4 hours with your library card. A laptop returned late costs 50 rupees for each started hour.
Headphones and phone chargers can be borrowed from the front desk for the day.

## Study rooms

There are 12 group study rooms on the second floor of the Central Library. Each room holds up
to 6 people and has a whiteboard and a screen. Book a room through the library portal up to
7 days in advance, for at most 2 hours per day. If your group does not arrive within 15
minutes, the booking is cancelled. The silent reading hall on the third floor has 200 seats;
talking and phone calls are not allowed there.

## Printing and scanning

Printers and scanners are on the ground floor. Black-and-white printing costs 2 rupees per
page and colour printing costs 10 rupees per page. Scanning to email is free. Pay with your
library card, which you can top up at the front desk or online.

## Interlibrary loans

If a book is not in our collection, ask for an interlibrary loan at the Research Help Desk.
Most books arrive within 10 working days. Interlibrary loans are free for postgraduate
students; undergraduate students pay 50 rupees per request.

## Research help

Librarians at the Research Help Desk on the first floor help with finding sources, citing
them, and using the online databases. You can also book a 30-minute one-to-one session with a
subject librarian through the library portal. Workshops on reference managers such as Zotero
are held every second Wednesday at 3 pm.
